How to read this

The benchmark intentionally keeps the first version as the reference point.

Average FPS

Overall throughput across measured frames after warmup.

1% low

FPS derived from the slowest one percent of frames. Higher means fewer noticeable hitches.

P95 frame time

Ninety-five percent of frames completed at or below this cost. Lower is better.

CPU-observed frame intervals measured between completed Present calls. This captures application-side throughput and driver back-pressure; it is not a GPU timestamp profile.
