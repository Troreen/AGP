How to read this

Confidence intervals quantify uncertainty in the run-level arithmetic mean.

Throughput

Average FPS is the primary throughput measure. Higher is better.

Tail behavior

1% low FPS and P95/P99 frame time expose hitching and slow frames.

Consistency

Sample standard deviation and coefficient of variation describe run-to-run stability.

Interpretation rules

A better mean is not sufficient by itself.

Improved

The effect is practically meaningful, repeatable, and larger than the observed uncertainty.

Neutral

The difference is small relative to the confidence interval or run-to-run variation.

Regressed

The measured version is meaningfully worse after uncertainty and variation are considered.

CPU-observed frame intervals measured between completed Present calls. Present time is diagnostic evidence, not a GPU timestamp or whole-process CPU profile. Raw frames.csv and summary.json files remain the source evidence.
